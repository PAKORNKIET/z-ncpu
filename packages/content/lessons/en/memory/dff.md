# D Flip-Flop

Computers step in time with a **clock**. A D Flip-Flop stores d **only at the rising edge** of clk (when it goes from 0 to 1); at all other times q stays the same.

In the tests, "⏱" means one clock pulse (0 → 1 → 0). While experimenting, use the **⏱ clock** button in the right panel or flip the clk switch yourself. Hint: two D Latches in a row, enabled on opposite clock levels.
